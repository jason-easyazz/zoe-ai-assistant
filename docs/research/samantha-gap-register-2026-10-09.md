## 1. In plain words: the ten gaps a household member would feel, ranked

Ranked by how often a person in the house would notice it times how well the evidence pins it down. Each one is something no
in-flight PR closes by itself; where an in-flight PR helps, the line says what is left over.

**1. She does not yet know the thread of your life.** The night mind that would let her say "On Monday you said the knee was sore, how is it today?" exists
but is off, and its own cells are still red on the small brain. Evidence: `ZOE_NIGHT_MIND` defaults off (`night_mind.py:41`); the four day-sim asks that need it
(1b, 2, 7b, S9c) all SKIP (`samantha_day_sim_trend.jsonl`, 06:28 run); on the live 4B it fails K6, K9, K9f, K10, K11, K12 (NM:67), on the 12B K9, K9f (and K10, K12 if the table is right) (`2026-10-09-cells.md`).
The field's evidence says the delivery matters as much as the memory: a 4-month, 40-person study found 78.8 percent recall accuracy with only 7.9 percent natural use in conversation, no satisfaction gain from QA accuracy
and a drop of about 0.5 SD for mistimed recalls (MemUse, [ext F] https://arxiv.org/html/2608.24189v1). *What closes it:* land PR 1955, run the pass in `shadow` for a week on the owner's real days, then serve it only after the two-week household tap (U1, section 4).

**2. She cannot tell who is talking.** The panel is a room. The speaker gate runs in shadow and its verdict gates nothing; the shadow week needs the owner's re-enrolment and a labelled TV and radio set, and today's targets are met only on pseudo-labels (OP:45).
Restraint therefore treats every unconfirmed voice as a guest (BP:739; `docs/knowledge/restraint.md:121`), which makes her guarded with the owner, and the owner's health news is one unconfirmed voice away from a visitor. Alexa and Google both put a per-person Voice ID in front of memory, reminders and kid filters
([ext F] https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/devices/new-alexa-top-features, [ext F] https://support.google.com/googlehome/answer/16618650?hl=en). *What closes it:* re-enrol, run the labelled shadow week, then feed the verdict into restraint, ingest and exact-words (U2).

**3. She mishears names and wakes to the wrong things, and nobody has a rate for either.** There is no wake false-accept or false-reject figure, only near-miss log lines, and the Pi runs the ONNX backend of a classifier trained on TFLite features (OP:128); a TV false wake is a known corpus class (VP:125).
The voice path hardcodes `confidence=1.0`, so a garbled name is acted on rather than checked (EP:955, "W1.5 conversational repair, NOT STARTED", EP:1052), and Moonshine key-term biasing for household names is plumbed and off (OP:127). Home Assistant's own 2025-2026 voice work added two wake words per satellite, a local fuzzy matcher and "ask a question, match the answer against a list"
([ext F] https://www.home-assistant.io/blog/2025/10/22/voice-chapter-11/, [ext F] https://www.home-assistant.io/integrations/assist_satellite/). *What closes it:* measure wake FA/FR on a household negative set, add one cheap "did you say X?" repair on low-confidence names, bias the household's names.

**4. She cannot be interrupted gracefully, and a cough or "mm-hm" can kill her sentence.** Barge-in cancels playback on speech; the duck, decide, resume path (BV1) has never been run in the lab and is flag-dark (OP:44; BP:607). The panel lane has no overlap labels and no resume (BP:607). The field treats this as solved plumbing: LiveKit pauses on voice activity, waits a timeout for a transcript, and resumes the paused speech if none arrives (a cough, noise), with an adaptive classifier for real barge-in vs backchannel ([ext F] https://livekit.com/blog/turn-detection-and-interruption-handling); MaAI offers a CPU, MIT-licensed backchannel and turn predictor ([ext F] https://github.com/maai-kyoto/maai); Smart Turn v3.2 (8 MB, CPU, 40 percent fewer misclassified short replies) is already vendored for the LiveKit lane ([ext F] https://www.daily.co/blog/smart-turn-v3-2-handling-noisy-environments-and-short-responses/; VP:1360).
*What closes it:* the owner runs the room lab for phase 1; no Orin RAM is needed (it is a Pi-only change).

**5. Her manner is not yet a person's.** The bench cells for the manner are inconclusive, not passing: on the 2026-10-09 baseline, reflective listening 15 of 20 against a bar of 16, the planted flaw named 7 of 20 against 15, a good plan praised 7 of 10 against 16 of 20, the due loop voiced 4 of 10 against 8 (SP:247, SP:239, SP:241, SP:256).
The live brain still carries the fixed soul that says "share a take" and has no restraint line (BP:49); the household persona layer is off and wired only to the dormant lane (`persona_layer.py:1`), and the 155-token manner block (BP2/`PERSON_DOCTRINE`) is not in the tree. The bench's oracle arm already showed that part of the person half is a prompt property the 4B can execute (P2.b 0 of 20 to 20 of 20, SP:271).
The field lesson is that tone is the product: Pi's listening-first manner was its differentiator and reviewers say added task features diluted it ([ext S] https://aicompanionguides.com/blog/what-happened-to-pi-ai-2026/); a 2,000-token compact memory on a 4B beat frontier models at implicit preferences in PersonaMem-v2 ([ext F] https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.06688). Sycophancy is the failure to design against: models preserve the user's face 45 points more than humans ([ext F] https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.13995) and a naive thumbs-up signal caused OpenAI's April 2025 rollback ([ext F] https://venturebeat.com/ai/openai-rolls-back-chatgpts-sycophancy-and-explains-what-went-wrong). *What closes it:* build the manner block behind the envelope flag, run `none / system / oracle`, keep it only if the three arms separate.

**6. "Forget it" is not yet total.** Forgetting scores 18 of 21 and poisoning 18 of 21 on Zoe's own memory (BK:30, BK:33). Forgotten text still sits in `chat_messages` and in plaintext compaction backups (OP:78), a misspelt name ("Marisal") survives the exact-token ledger (OP:77), a digest can resurrect a forgotten name when the 300 s tombstone expires (OP:86), and the phone numbers and door codes the night quote gate would keep are not scrubbed (OP:188).
Selective forgetting and knowledge update are the weakest axis across published memory systems (MemoryAgentBench: conflicting-fact single-hop 45 to 54 percent for most methods, multi-hop at most 28 percent, [ext F] https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.05257), so this is where a household would first lose trust. *What closes it:* the W1 forget build (redact `chat_messages` at forget time, alias sweep by edit distance confirmed by voice, a backup policy), then re-run the F cells.

**7. The voice panel behaves worse than typed chat.** The text lane got the correction, contacts-conversation and verify-on-challenge tiers; the voice lane did not (OP:98), and the panel is the voice-first surface (VISION principle 8). "No, that's wrong, it's Thursday" is applied in chat and not by voice, and a spoken "that's wrong" writes no feedback row at all (voice feedback is NOT STARTED, EP:1058; the only writer of `chat_feedback` is the chat route, `routers/chat.py:3365`). *What closes it:* lane-parity cells in the replay corpus, then port the three tiers behind the replay gate.

**8. She does not keep her own promises.** "I'll remind you" or "I'll keep an eye on it" creates no row and no trigger (W2.5 follow-through, NOT STARTED, EP:1053; no commitment code in `services/zoe-data`), and what she herself said last week is recoverable only in an in-process ledger that a restart empties (OP:69, OP:156). Nothing in the bar scores a kept promise.
*What closes it:* a commitment row written from her own reply, a deterministic check-and-report trigger, and a cell that asks "did you do what you said?"

**9. Children and the dark hours.** There is no crisis path in the tree (ES:161-163), no kid mode (a minor receives the fixed persona, `persona_layer.py:442`; ES:86), and the 12B night takes the voice down for 19 minutes even in cells-only mode (`2026-10-09-cells.md`, wake table) and about 85 minutes in the planned window (BP:479), exactly when an insomniac or an unwell child is up.
The in-flight distress hand-off covers detection; it does not cover a minor, the phone lane, or a person who speaks to a sleeping panel. The field has moved on minors: Alexa and Google lock child profiles to filtered answers that a parent cannot loosen ([ext F] https://support.google.com/googlehome/answer/16916838?hl=en), Character.AI ended open-ended chat for under-18s on 2025-11-24 ([ext F] https://blog.character.ai/an-update-on-changes-to-our-under-18-experience/), California SB 243 requires a suicide-ideation protocol for known minors ([ext S] https://www.troutmanprivacy.com/2026/01/analyzing-the-new-ai-companion-chatbot-laws/).
*What closes it:* after the hand-off ships, write the kid-mode rules the safety note already fixes, add a one-line "Zoe is resting, call 000" answer for a sleeping panel, and make the 4B night (no downtime) the default until the 12B earns its silence (G19/G20 below).

**10. She is one room with one flat voice.** There is no cross-surface thread (a thing said to the panel is not known to Telegram, EP:1057), no outbound voice note or remote live voice (EP:1042-1044), and the ear-tuned, replay-green expressive delivery is off (`voice_delivery.py:103`, FI:165; EP:1012-1040).
Google keeps a 15-minute conversation across devices and a "Home Brief" recap while away ([ext F] https://9to5google.com/2026/07/23/nest-cam-july-2026-update/, [ext F] https://support.google.com/googlehome/answer/15962877?hl=en); Alexa+ continues a conversation across Echo, phone and car ([ext F] https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/devices/new-alexa-top-features).
*What closes it:* flip the expressive profile (half a day, ear-verified), then a per-user active-thread block, then Telegram voice notes; unprompted speech stays off by the owner's decision.

*Not felt until it fails, but ranked in the register:* the private stack is reachable on the LAN and the bot token has reached a journal (G24), nothing has ever been restored from backup (G25), and nobody can say how often she is simply down (G26).

## 4. The three biggest unknowns, and the smallest honest experiment for each

**U1. Is what she brings up welcome, or creepy, in this house?** Everything in G01, G02 and the "I noticed" demo (D2) rests on it, and no lab cell can answer it: the strongest published signal says
mistimed recall costs satisfaction and system-initiated recall adds none, in a 40-person study (MemUse), and the only trust experiment is simulated shopping chats. *Experiment:* the blueprint's E10 cut to its core. Two adults who agree, 14 days, `ZOE_NIGHT_MIND=shadow` and the delivery ledger on in shadow, nothing served; each morning the phone shows the one line Zoe *would* have raised and three buttons (true, false, creepy).
No typing on the panel. Pre-registered stop: any "creepy" on a raise line stops serving; go only if at least 95 percent "true" and at least 80 percent "I'd want that" (BP:796, E10). Cost: 4 days to build the phone card, 14 calendar days.

**U2. Who is actually speaking in the shared room, and what happens when it is wrong?** Restraint, the guest rule, forgetting scope, poisoning and any kid mode all lean on a speaker verdict that has never been measured on real labels (OP:45); the TV, the radio and a child's voice are the likely false-accepts.
*Experiment:* re-enrol the owner (8 prompts x 3 conditions), then one shadow week with a scripted negative set recorded at the panel: 20 clips each of the TV, a podcast, a child, a visitor saying "Zoe, what did I say about my health?". Log the verdict only. Report false-accept per negative class with Wilson intervals and the owner's false-reject on 50 real turns; the thresholds (0.70 voice, 0.45 face) are the existing ones. No behaviour changes in the experiment.

**U3. Can the night pass read a *real* day, on which engine, in how long?** Every night figure is a synthetic household (K cells) or a 17-call smoke; the day size, the routine-command share and the share of ordinary-sounding turns the 4B under-selects (NM:67: "a run with a friend, a knee that stopped hurting") are unknown, and the 12B is 15 times slower than the 4B at the measured rates.
*Experiment:* E1 census first (counts only: owner turns, tokens, routine share, chunks, no text). Then `zoe-night-mind.py --dry-run` (nothing written) over the owner's last 7 days on the 4B, and once on a Sunday in the 12B window over the same days; the owner labels 20 proposed observations per engine true or false and 10 days of "what did it miss?" (free recall of three things that mattered). Decision rule: the 12B earns 20 minutes of silence only if it adds at least 15 points on the owner-labelled recall or lifts the K1 Wilson lower bound by 0.05 (BP:826, E8's own bar).

## 5. Verdict

Is this system good enough to be Samantha's brain today? As a *record* and a *rulebook*, yes, and measurably so: the cabinet keeps what the owner said above what any model inferred (authority 237 of 237, identity 39 of 39, abstention 12 of 12), reads the owner's own words back with the date (exact words 40 of 40 items), reaches two facts at distance, forgets on request in 18 of 21 cells, and the bar's 23 scenarios stand at 20 PASS, 1 FAIL (S10) and 2 SKIP when the latest result for each is taken (not one combined run, see the ground section).
As a *person*, no: the thread-of-your-life (G01), the manner (G10) and the shared-room identity (G03) are either off, unbuilt or unmeasured, the guards that fix holding a fact, asking which Marisol, and a clean goodbye are written and replay-green but ship in `shadow`, and the one number that decides whether any of her initiative is wanted (G02) has never been collected.
The shortest path is not more building; it is, in order: one full instrument run on one commit with a recorded baseline (G27, 2 days), the `shadow`-to-`enforce` flips for the person-half guards with their live re-runs (in flight), the speaker re-enrolment and shadow week (G03, one calendar week, in parallel), the 4B night in `shadow` on the owner's real days with the census (G01/G20, one calendar week), then the two-week household tap (G02/U1) before anything the night mind found is spoken, while the manner block (G10) is built in the gap and the expressive-voice flip (G08, half a day) lands as the first thing a person can *hear*.
That is roughly five weeks of calendar and about 25 working days of agent work, ahead of the 12B (G19/G20) and ahead of children (G17), which should wait for the distress hand-off and the owner's decisions 6.4, 6.6, 6.7 and 6.9 (G34). The 12B night is the owner's stated priority; the evidence here is that it is the most expensive way to find out something the 4B night and a 20-label census can already answer, and that it silences the house for the same hours a distress path would be needed.
