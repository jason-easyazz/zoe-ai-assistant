## 1. Platform by platform (what a sideloaded app can do in 2026)

### Android TV / Google TV (the only real candidate)
- Install: any APK via `adb install` after Developer options (tap Build x7) + USB/Wireless debugging; Android 14 needs the "Pair device with pairing code" flow (`adb pair ip:port`, then `adb connect`) [P/S] https://www.celsoazevedo.com/replace-launcher-chromecast-google-tv/ (author needed retries; older guides fail on Android 14).
- TV app manifest: `LEANBACK_LAUNCHER` intent filter, `uses-feature android.software.leanback required=false`, `android.hardware.touchscreen required=false`, 320x180 banner [P] https://developer.android.com/training/tv/start/start . Without LEANBACK_LAUNCHER the app does not appear in the TV UI even when sideloaded [P]. That page says nothing about HOME.
- Becoming HOME: there is NO supported "Default launcher" picker on Google TV. Status by evidence:
  - Google TV (Android 12+): Home button always goes to `com.google.android.apps.tv.launcherx`; a third-party app declaring `category.HOME` + `category.DEFAULT` is selectable only after the stock launcher is disabled via ADB: `pm disable-user --user 0 com.google.android.apps.tv.launcherx` and `com.google.android.tungsten.setupwraith` (setupwraith otherwise acts as fallback and re-enables the stock launcher) [S] https://www.celsoazevedo.com/replace-launcher-chromecast-google-tv/ , https://gist.github.com/SMUsamaShah/25a1c4958fcdfe87db49da18f66dbf7a . Revert: `pm enable --user 0 <pkg>`.
  - Older Android TV: `com.google.android.tvlauncher`; Fully's documented sequence: `pm set-home-activity <pkg>/<activity>` + `pm disable-user --user 0 com.google.android.tvlauncher`; revert with `pm enable` and `pm set-home-activity com.google.android.tvlauncher/.MainActivity` [P] https://www.fully-kiosk.com/docs/en/faq/tv . Fully warns: "these settings could be reverted by the next Android TV update" and it only works on Google-compliant devices [P].
  - No-ADB workarounds: Button Mapper remaps Home; Launcher Manager; Kyu/Projectivy via Accessibility service [S] https://www.xda-developers.com/replacing-google-tv-home-screen-with-projectivy-launcher/ . Reliability reports: Projectivy sometimes fails to load after power cycle or reverts until accessibility is toggled [S].
  - Side effects reported on Chromecast with Google TV after disabling the stock launcher: YouTube remote button stops; disabling setupwraith can forget saved Wi-Fi on some units; stock launcher may handle HDMI input switching, so test it [S].
  - Google 2024-2026 restrictions on launcher replacement: I found NO primary source of a new hard block on Google TV [U]. The ecosystem (Projectivy, Free My TV, Cretezy Oct 2026 guide https://cretezy.com/2026/10/ad-free-android-tv ) still works as of Oct 2026 [S]. Persistence across OTA is the open risk (Fully warns; no source says it resets) [U].
- Mic: RECORD_AUDIO is a normal runtime permission; background capture is blocked on API 28+ so the app must be foreground (a HOME launcher that is the foreground app qualifies) [P] https://developer.android.com/media/platform/mediarecorder . Far-field "hands-free" mics: Google said 2025 Google TV hardware adds far-field mics + proximity sensors; Gemini for TV launched 2025-09-22 on TCL QM9K, Android TV OS 14+, initial TCL-only, US-only; June 2026 hands-free settings control rolling out [S] https://www.androidauthority.com/gemini-on-google-tv-release-timeline-device-availability-3556477 , https://chromeunboxed.com/gemini-for-google-tv-is-gaining-hands-free-settings-control-for-tv-hardware/ . Whether those array mics are exposed to a third-party app (vs held by the Google voice stack) is NOT documented anywhere I could reach; Fire TV reports silent AudioRecord [S/U]. Treat as "test on the specific set; do not design around it". Consistent with our stance: ears stay on the Pi.
- Screensaver/Daydream: since a 2022 Google TV update there is no UI to pick a third-party screensaver, but `settings put secure screensaver_components <pkg>/<DreamService>` still works; default on Google TV is `com.google.android.apps.tv.dreamx/.service.Backdrop` (note it before changing) [S] https://github.com/theothernt/aerialviews . So the estate's ambient face can be a DreamService (WebView in a Dream) with no launcher replacement at all. This is the lowest-risk first rung.
- HDMI-CEC from an app: HdmiControlManager is a system/privileged API [U, my knowledge]; a normal app cannot send arbitrary CEC. Practical CEC: a streaming stick wakes the TV and takes the active source via CEC on its own wake (One Touch Play) [U], and HA can wake the stick over the Android TV Remote protocol. On a TV with Google TV built in (TCL), CEC is irrelevant: wake the set itself (HA doc: TCL may need "Screenless service", Xiaomi/TCL may go unavailable when off) [P].

### LG webOS
- Developer Mode app + `ares-cli`; installs packaged IPK or hosted web app [S]. Session is time-limited and shown in "Remain Session"; EXTEND works only while networked and only before expiry; dev mode is disabled after 10 reboots offline or after a reboot once the session ran out, and installed dev apps are then uninstalled [P] https://webostv.developer.lge.com/develop/getting-started/developer-mode-app . (Older README said 50 h inactivity [S]; the page gave no hours.) No third-party mic API found. Homebrew Channel/root: as of 2025-08-24 latest firmware for webOS 5/6/7/9 is patched; 2025 models only vulnerable if still on factory webOS 10.0; do not update firmware if you want root [S] https://github.com/throwaway96/dejavuln-autoroot . No Home-screen replacement. Verdict: display via a webOS app is possible but needs a keepalive cron to EXTEND; flaky.

### Samsung Tizen
- Developer mode via Apps screen key sequence + reboot; build with Tizen Studio/`tizen` CLI; needs author + per-TV distributor certificate; certificate lifetime unclear (a 30-day claim from 2022 is dated) [S] https://samsungtizenos.com/knowledge-hub/extending-tizen-certificate-duration-tv-app-10100 . Firmware updates can force reinstall; USB widget install no longer supported [S]. No mic info found. Verdict: display only, re-sign periodically. URL Launcher style apps exist [S].

### Fire TV
- Sideload easy (Downloader). Launcher replacement: Fire OS 8.1.6.9+ (Sept 2025) reportedly blocks usual methods; XDA "Home on Fire" uses an accessibility-service workaround, not a true default [S] https://xdaforums.com/t/app-firetv-noroot-home-on-fire-use-any-launcher-as-your-fire-tv-home-home-button-boot-fire-os-8-7.4791567/ . Vega OS (announced 2025-09-30) has no sideloading [S]. HA androidtv_remote does not support Fire TV (ADB integration does) [P]. Verdict: avoid.

### Roku: no sideloaded apps without dev-mode channel with a 500-ish limit; no HOME; skip [U, my knowledge].
### Apple tvOS: TestFlight/Xcode only, no launcher replacement, mic only on the Siri Remote [U, my knowledge; no source fetched]. The Apple split (HomePod = ears, Apple TV = screen) is the model to copy conceptually.

## 4. Build and deploy path from the Jetson (aarch64, no Mac)
- Jetson is aarch64: Google's build-tools (aapt2) ship x86_64 Linux only [U], so BUILD IN GitHub Actions on an ubuntu runner (x86_64): `sdkmanager` cmdline-tools, Gradle, Kotlin; output a signed APK artifact. Sign with a local release keystore kept on the box (never in the repo; only the CI debug-sign or a secret-injected key; keep the keystore in the vault/secret topology).
- Install/update without adb binary: HA's androidtv integration uses pure-Python `adb-shell` [P], so the box can run `adb_shell`/`adb-shell` from Python for `install`, `pm`, `am start`; or apt `adb` on arm64 [U]. Pairing on Android 14: wireless-debugging pair code is needed once; the key then persists (Cretezy/Celso: "Always allow") [S].
- ADB exposure: wireless debugging listens on the LAN to anyone who pairs; a paired key is persistent. Keep it off except during updates by turning Wireless debugging off after install (Celso advises this) [S]; better, have the launcher app self-update (download APK from the box over the LAN, install via PackageInstaller with the "install unknown apps" grant) so ADB stays off day to day [U]. Never leave ADB on a port forwarded to the internet.
- Minimal app (Kotlin, ~300 lines): manifest with `MAIN`+`HOME`+`DEFAULT`+`LEANBACK_LAUNCHER` on a single activity; fullscreen WebView loading `http://<box>/touch/home.html`; JS-enabled, DOM storage, WebSocket-friendly; inject `X-Device-Token` via `WebViewClient.shouldInterceptRequest` or load a URL with token cookie set by `/api/panels/provision` flow; keep-screen-on; reconnect/reload on network loss; `startLockTask` is only useful with device-owner (avoid) so rely on HOME + no back-exit; D-pad: map OK/Back to nothing (display-only). Add a DreamService variant for the screensaver rung. Crash watchdog idea from Free My TV (revert to stock after 3 crashes).

## 5. Zoe-side design
- New panel role `display`: provisioning reuse `panel_provision.py` (code pairing; /confirm requires a signed-in member) and `panel_auth.py` (device tokens, SHA-256 hashed, renewable). TV has no typing, so QR/code on the TV screen is shown and the owner confirms on the phone (matches VISION principle 8: connecting devices = QR on screen, finish on phone). Scope: read-only estate feeds; no PIN pad route (PIN pad is the one allowed typed exception, not on a TV), no voice/mic claims, no identity-step-up; token role cannot mint sessions.
- Privacy: a TV is a shared screen. Treat as guest-level surface: render private cards as absence (HA-Family-Hub pattern in IDEAS), never personal calendars/mail/health; "go private" ungated. Show private results only on the Pi panel after identity (VISION: voice-verified short panel session).
- "Show on the TV": brain tool `show_on_display(target, card)` + a card action; backend pushes over the existing WebSocket to panels with role display (needs the multi-panel targeting IDEAS is already waiting on). Wake: HA `androidtv_remote` turn_on (or `remote.turn_on`) -> stick wakes -> CEC wakes TV [U]; input switch: stick as active source implies the input; for built-in Google TV skip.
- Idle: the estate ambient face as DreamService/launcher idle; power policy: blank when nobody is home; do not burn-in OLED.
- Audio: keep TTS on the Pi panel/room speaker. Routing TTS to the TV adds Cast/HDMI-ARC latency and lip-sync risk and loses barge-in coherence; if wanted later, play via Music Assistant to the TV's Chromecast player (already drives Chromecast) [U, design call]. Ears stay on Pi.

## 7. Risks
1. OTA resets launcher/enabled packages (Fully's own warning) -> keep a detect-and-reapply job (HA ADB check `dumpsys package` home activity) and ensure the fallback is the stock launcher, never a blank screen.
2. Play Protect may flag a sideloaded launcher; mitigate by signing consistently and install via ADB [U].
3. ADB left on (LAN-wide debug access); see section 4.
4. ACR/telemetry: the video-style debloat uses `pm disable-user` only (reversible); do not disable Google services, inputs, remote pairing, Play Store (Free My TV PROTECTED list [P via blog]). ACR on TCL is separate from Google TV; turn off via Settings > privacy rather than package surgery [U].
5. WebView version on non-Google-certified or old sets [U].
6. Licence: do not copy FLauncher (GPL-3.0) or Projectivy (proprietary) code; MIT Free My TV is mineable with attribution.
7. Dev-mode TVs (webOS/Tizen) expire: avoid as a primary path.
