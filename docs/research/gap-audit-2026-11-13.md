# Whole-system gap audit, 2026-11-13

Owner ask: "look at everything, even our ui, whats missing, what doesnt work, what features have no ui, what features are missing,
we will eventually have to feed calendars, mail, etc etc into this, what fields might be missing".

## How to read this

- Five read-only sub-agent lanes ran on 2026-11-13: panel (touch) UI, desktop and phone UI, feature-to-surface map, data model for
  feeds, vision vs reality. No lane drove a browser, ran tests or called the chat API. No household content was read: schema, counts
  and log marker counts only.
- Evidence tags: [code] = read in the repo at a file:line; [live] = a read-only check on the box; [unverified] = inferred.
- The lane files hold the file:line evidence for every line here. This record is the ranked summary; ROADMAP.md still owns the order
  of work. Nothing here has been re-verified by a second reader except where it says so.
- Security and privacy gaps are described by class only.

## 1. The fifteen things that matter most

Ranked by how much each stands between today and the experience the owner wants.

1. **She learns almost nothing new.** [live] The pendant produced 106 candidate facts; all are pending and none was ever offered,
   because the day review is in shadow and its raise step is off. `member_register`, `now_cards`, `conversation_notes`,
   `learned_voiceprints` and `person_important_dates` hold 0 rows. No external calendar is connected.
2. **Memory rarely reaches a reply.** [live] Every-turn recall and the recall gate are in shadow. The 2026-11-11 research measured
   memory used in 2 of 32 replies. Household traffic is too thin to prove these flags; the proof has to come from the harness.
3. **Memory is wrong in ways people notice.** QA round 8: a corrected value does not retire the old one, a stored fact is denied,
   money amounts are dropped, a forget said before filing does not hold. Fixes are in two PRs that are not live yet.
4. **No member can see or delete what Zoe knows about them from a screen.** [code] The desktop memories page's add button always
   fails (wrong method), and the "what Zoe knows about me" panels are behind flags that are off. Export and opt-out are API-only.
5. **The panel needs a keyboard it does not have.** [code] Nine dialogs on the live home screen require typing (list rename,
   reminder title, contact edit, link a person, create a room, panel location, dock label, playlist name).
6. **Failures are silent, on screen and off.** [code] The home screen has no "cannot reach Zoe" state, 41 swallowed errors and about
   20 requests with no time limit; several taps flash success and then fail. [live] A messaging bot crash-looped for about nine hours
   on 2026-11-07 and nothing told the owner. Zoe never says "my music link is down".
7. **Empty transcriptions.** [live] 2,137 in eight days against about 12 real voice turns a day. Either false wakes or dropped
   commands; unmeasured.
8. **Nothing reaches a phone.** [live] Web push has 0 subscriptions and the messaging channel does not carry reminders. Reminders and
   proactive lines never leave the panel.
9. **No connected-accounts foundation.** [code] No accounts table, no token store, and the phone hand-off accepts music only. The
   calendar and contacts tables already point at an accounts table that does not exist. This blocks every feed.
10. **Forget does not reach everything.** [code] Calendar, reminders, lists, notes, journal and transactions are listed gaps in the
    forget-reach record. A synced calendar would survive "forget me". Saved voice audio and rotated raw logs are also out of reach.
11. **Owner jobs need a terminal.** [code] Of 31 owner tasks checked, 14 work from a screen, 5 have a broken or placebo screen, 2 are
    hidden behind off flags and 10 need a terminal, the API or the panel. No screen can mark a member as a child, although the child
    safety rules depend on that role.
12. **Calendar is not ready for a synced feed.** [code] The read API matches on start date only (a multi-day event that began before
    the window vanishes), repeating events are never expanded outside anticipation, cancelled events are read out as real, and voice
    edits can change an event the provider owns.
13. **Built and tested, switched off.** [live] 25 capabilities are off and 10 are in shadow, including web search (so "are you
    sure?" and live lookups cannot work). Section 3 lists them.
14. **Backups and recovery.** [live] No off-box copy. The restore drill has been red since 2026-11-08 (the data restores; a missing
    owner step fails the drill). Ten of the last sixteen night windows completed.
15. **Development takes her offline.** [live] The main service restarted about 140 times in six days and the speech service about
    326 times; voice is silent during each.

  [code, not run].

False promises [code]:

- A voice page calls a confirm route that does not exist.
- The distress hand-off says the household contact is told; no contact is configured and the notify step is skipped when empty.

Still pointing at retired agents [code]: the delegate routes, an agent task list the desktop chat polls, four "build" intents, and

## 6. Feeds: what the data model needs

Headline: for calendar and contacts most FIELDS already exist; the plumbing around them does not.

What exists [live]: calendar storage and contacts storage are built with external ids, etags, sync cursors, tombstones and per-field
provenance, and cover most of the iCalendar and vCard fields. Both are empty on the box (no external calendars or contact sources; 18
local events). The live schema matches the migrations (column names compared, not types).

What is missing, worst first:

1. **Accounts and tokens.** No `connected_accounts` table, no token store. Reuse the phone hand-off and the messaging-link screen
   pattern for "QR on the panel, finish on the phone".
2. **Forget reach** for calendar data and for reminders, lists, notes, journal, transactions.
3. **One calendar read path with recurrence expansion.** Today each reader queries events itself and ignores status, read-only and
   timezone.
4. **A memory class for "a provider said so"**, below user-stated and confirmed, with a pointer back to the source item, so "where
   did you hear that" and forget-by-source work and an inferred fact never overrides a stated one.
5. **Provenance on the tables that lack it**: reminders, lists, list items, notes, journal, transactions (source, external id, etag).
6. **Time.** Stored four ways: 238 text columns, 22 epoch floats, 13 naive timestamps, 2 timezone-aware. The household zone is one
   setting; there is no per-member timezone.
7. **A member's own addresses and numbers** (`member_identities`), so an invited attendee or a mail recipient can be recognised as a
   household member.
8. **Tasks and lists sync fields**: due instant, completed-at, cancelled status, item notes, external id, delete timestamp.
9. **Sync run state**: a run log, backoff, a disconnect fence, idempotent import (the .ics importer always inserts), one delete
   convention (three are in use).
10. **Mail has nothing**: no table of any kind.

Staged plan (each item is marked needed-now or later in the lane file):

- **Stage 0, plumbing.** `connected_accounts`, `account_secrets`, `sync_runs`, `member_identities`; the `external_source` memory
  class; forget reaching calendar data.
- **Stage 1, read-only calendar.** One shared read/write path with recurrence expansion; import that updates instead of duplicating;
  three event columns (`provider_updated_at`, `content_hash`, `account_generation`); source shown in the UI; Edit and Delete hidden on
  read-only events.
- **Stage 2, contacts.** Wire the accounts table; several phones and emails per person in the UI; a source badge.
- **Stage 3, mail.** Metadata-only `mail_items` with no body column, a sync-state table, and proposals the member accepts before
  anything from mail becomes an event, a list item or a memory. Never stored locally: bodies, attachment bytes, bcc lists, tokens
  outside the vault.

RAM, not disk, is the limit on this box, so feed bodies stay out of the vector memory store.

## 7. Reliability and operations

[live, counts only]
- Alerting covers the main service only, and its channel is the bot that can itself be down.
- Alarms failed silently during a database pool exhaustion on 2026-11-05/06 (149 tick failures), with no catch-up and no notice.
- Night window: 10 of 16 completed; on one night a killed window re-reported an earlier night's result as ok.
- Brain failover is off; the brain restarted 15 times in about six days.
- Hygiene by class: many environment-file backup copies sit beside the live one, one with loose permissions; QA chats are counted as
  household traffic (60 of 290 turns), which inflates usage figures.
- Usage: a median of about 17 household turns a day. Lists, weather, people lookup, calendar and memory are used; music by voice,
  timers, notes, journal, intercom and named guests are essentially unused.

## 8. Docs that are false today

About 20 claims, including: plans saying the night-mind readers are off (they serve), kids' enrolment off (it is on), the README
saying the night brain is not yet nightly (it is), the distress hand-off claim above, and the event-loop item marked done while
stalls continue. `open-problems.md` has 574 lines, 33 marked resolved but not deleted and 486 older than 21 days. Twelve problems
recur across audits without being fixed, among them off-box backup and the size of the main voice router file.

## 9. Inputs for ROADMAP.md

Not an order of work: `ROADMAP.md` is the one ordered list. These are the groups of work this audit points at, unordered, for the
owner to place there.
