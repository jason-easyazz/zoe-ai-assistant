# Cold wake warm-ups (2026-11-02)

Ask (usage audit 2026-11-02, PR #2235): 13 of 78 real panel turns had a first word over 3 s; 6 of 10 wake warm-ups
were a cold prefill and the real turn queued behind it.

## Measured (read-only; zoe-data log 2026-10-26..11-02 joined to `journalctl --user -u llama-server`)

26 wake warms actually ran (another 143 wakes were `kv=skipped:hot`: a turn ran in the last 180 s, prefix resident).

| outcome | n | warm cost |
|---|---|---|
| hit (`first_cache_n` 2.6-3.3k, `prompt_n` 13-33) | 12 | 0.25-1.1 s (one 6 s: the card-version repeat) |
| partial (older envelope, `cache_n` 2154) | 2 | 1.0-1.1 s |
| **cold** (`first_cache_n` 0-6, `prompt_n` 2.7-3.3k) | **12** | **4.2-5.8 s** |

What ran on the slot between the last conversation and each cold warm (journal tasks; small = under 500 prompt tokens):

- 10 of 12 had **4-30 helper-sized prompts** since the last conversation (typically 12-26: the guarded batches
  `person_extractor_llm`, `turn_digest`, `latent_intent_detector`, `structural_*`, plus unguarded callers).
- 2 of 12 followed a **brain restart** (the nightly 12B window restarts the 4B at ~02:55; the first wake after is cold by
  construction).
- Hits survived 0-6 helper prompts, and even 10 h idle with 2-3 (the host-cache LRU keeps the entry), so the loss is by
  displacement, not by time.
- **Two mechanisms leave it displaced:** (1) `brain_slot_guard` correctly defers helpers while a conversation is hot, but
  the batch then runs (`released_quiet`, or `released_max` 240 s after the call arrived: on 2026-11-01 14:57 that was
  150 s after the last turn, 11 helper prompts in 2 s) and nothing puts the prefix back; (2) callers outside the guard.
- **Did the real turn wait behind the warm?** In 9 of the 12 cold cases, yes: the command arrived 0-3 s (median ~1.8 s)
  into the warm and its TTFT was 3.3-8.2 s. That is still faster than a cold turn with no warm (~5.4 s prefill plus
  decode), so a warm that yields would not help: the prefill is the same work either way. The fix is not being cold. No
  hit warm overlapped a turn.

## Decision

- **Fix: `brain_prefix_keeper.py`** (`ZOE_BRAIN_PREFIX_KEEPER`, default on). Polls `GET /slots` every 15 s (no inference;
  `n_prompt_tokens` exists on b11194 and b11982). When the slot is idle and holds under
  `ZOE_BRAIN_PREFIX_KEEPER_MIN_TOKENS` (2000) for 8 s, the conversation is not hot, no helper is queued, the brain-window
  lock is free, a panel speaker was noted in the last 12 h and the Flue lane is on, it runs the same throwaway warm the
  wake runs (`zoe_flue_client.warm_kv_prefix`) for that speaker. Limits: 120 s between warms, 8 an hour, 5 min back-off
  after a failure. Cost: one ~5 s prefill when displaced, in a quiet period. Benefit: the next wake is a ~0.5 s hit or is
  skipped as hot. Log line `PREFIX_KEEPER outcome=... slot_tokens=...`.
- **Before/after estimate** (not yet measured live): if the keeper converts the 10 batch-displaced and 2 restart-displaced
  cases, cold wakes fall from 12/26 (46%) toward the few that follow an unguarded caller inside the 8 s settle window, and
  the affected first word drops by ~3-4 s (cold ~5.4 s to a ~1 s hit). Verify after deploy: `FLUE_KV_WARM` lines with
  `first_cache_n=0` per day, and `PREFIX_KEEPER outcome=warmed` counts.
- **Not done:** a warm that yields to the turn (no gain, above); holding helpers longer (the batch is cheap, the
  displacement is the cost); shortening `released_max` (a 28 s sliver in the one measured flush, and the keeper covers it);
  re-warming mid-conversation while hot (it would compete with the live conversation).

## Slot save/restore, checked against both builds

Not used. `POST /slots/0?action=save|restore` needs `--slot-save-path` (a unit change, owner-run). On **b11194**
(`tools/server/server-context.cpp:2545-2660`) it saves and loads `ctx_tgt` only: the MTP draft context is not saved, so a
restored slot has target KV and no draft KV (unsafe under `--spec-type draft-mtp`). On **b11982**
(`server-context.cpp:3276-3330`) it also saves checkpoints and the draft state. In both, SWA layers keep only the last 512
positions (`docs/research/brain-slot-isolation-2026-10-29.md` 3b): enough for the warm prefix (the next prompt diverges at
the tail) but exactly what the host cache already restores, so it only buys immunity from host-cache eviction. Restore
time was NOT measured: the box has ~0.5 GB available so a second server cannot be loaded, and the live brain cannot be
stopped in the day. The keeper reaches the same state with no flag. Revisit on b11982 (PR #2231) only if the keeper's hit
rate disappoints.

## Owner steps

None required. The keeper starts with zoe-data (restart to pick it up; it waits 30 s). Off switch:
`ZOE_BRAIN_PREFIX_KEEPER=0`. Voice-path change: lands through the replay gate.
